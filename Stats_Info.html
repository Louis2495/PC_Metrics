Metric Explanation Measured in
Active Users Number of unique users that had an engaged session # of Users
New Users Number of unique new users # of Users
Sessions Total number of visits to the site (not necessarily unique) # of Sessions
Avg Session Length Average session length across all (active and inactive) users Seconds
Pages per Session Average number of pages accessed per session (not necessarily unique) # of Pages
Engaged Sessions # of visits longer than 10 seconds, had at least 2 page views, or involved a key event # of Sessions
Engagement Rate The fraction of visits that were engaged sessions (Engaged Sessions / Total Sessions) Decimal
Bounce Rate The fraction of visits that were not engaged sessions (Non-engaged Sessions / Total Sessions) Decimal
Avg Engagement The average time the website was actively in use (Engagement Duration / Active Users) Seconds
